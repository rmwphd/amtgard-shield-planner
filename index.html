Face area is the largest flat projection of the shield. Width, height and area here are the finished size, including edge padding. With padding set, the solid line shows the core you cut before padding goes on.

When a target is set, the dimension marked Auto is solved for you and rounded down to the nearest 1/16 inch, so cutting to that number keeps you at or under the target.
